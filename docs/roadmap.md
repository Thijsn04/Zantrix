# Roadmap

Design sequence reconstructed from the handover. Stages after documentation recovery have not been implemented. No deadline has been agreed.

## Roadmap

## Stage 0: recover and preserve the written baseline

- Recover or reconstruct the documentation.
- Merge all pending answers.
- Verify links and consistency.
- Commit and push as authorized.
- Report the actual remote commit.

## Stage 1: review detailed foundation choices

Resolve:

- Module packaging.
- Core/Bridge contracts.
- Data ownership.
- Identity integration.
- Persistence boundaries.
- Shared frontend boundaries.
- Version compatibility.

## Stage 2: platform skeleton

Prove:

- A module can integrate without modifying Core.
- Invalid dependencies fail clearly.
- Security/context rules are enforced.
- UI contributions are coherent.
- A module has clear data ownership.

## Stage 3: complete GP consultation

Implement and test:

- Patient registration.
- Contact context.
- Documentation.
- SOEP through suitable modules.
- Drafts.
- Finalization/correction.
- Safe concurrency.
- Patient switching.
- Authorization.
- Synthetic scenarios.

## Stage 4: extensibility and interoperability proof

Exercise:

- Another care-setting scenario.
- Another national format or rule.
- A sample external module.
- A concrete Connect integration.
- Conflicting data.
- Retractions/corrections.
- Retries and failures.

## Stage 5: continuity and operation

Implement and verify:

- Local emergency reader.
- Synchronization.
- Offline identity.
- Backup/restore.
- Maintenance.
- Setup.
- Administration.
- Managed and self-hosted operation.

Architectural risks in this stage may need earlier focused investigations.

## Stage 6: full Dutch GP deployment scope

Validate:

- All critical practice workflows.
- National interfaces.
- Medication.
- Diagnostics.
- Correspondence.
- Administration.
- Migration.
- Patient access route.
- Clinical assurance.
- Security/privacy.
- Recovery.
- Applicable legal/regulatory obligations.

## Later expansion

- Own patient portal.
- Additional care settings.
- Additional countries.
- Broader integrations.
- Reviewed module catalog/marketplace.

See [open questions](open-questions.md) before planning implementation.
