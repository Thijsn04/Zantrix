# Security policy

Zantrix currently contains a documentation-only design baseline. The previous application's tests, dependencies and maturity statements do not validate the rebuild. No clinical readiness, certification, security assurance or supported production release is claimed.

## Reporting concerns

Do not publish suspected vulnerabilities, credentials, patient information or sensitive reproduction material in public issues, pull requests, screenshots or logs. Use synthetic data when describing a non-sensitive design concern.

No confidential security contact or enabled private vulnerability reporting channel has been verified for this baseline. A maintainer must establish and document a real confidential reporting route before security-sensitive deployment or marketplace operation. No response-time commitment or support lifecycle has been agreed. This document does not invent a reporting address or direct reporters to an unverified private channel.

## Design responsibilities

Security and privacy require explicit validation, operating procedures and ownership. Applicable regulatory responsibilities depend on intended use and jurisdiction and remain to be assessed; they cannot simply be assigned entirely to deploying institutions by this policy.

See [identity and sessions](docs/identity-access.md), [authorization and audit](docs/authorization.md), [clinical safety](docs/clinical-safety.md), [continuity](docs/continuity.md) and [governance](docs/governance.md). Offline identity, revocation, module trust, support access and backup security require further detailed design.
